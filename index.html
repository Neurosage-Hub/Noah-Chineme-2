03Two things to know

Energy and mold: two areas to look at more closely

Top priority

Very high ketones

476
3-HYDROXYBUTYRATE · REF 4.8
107
ACETOACETATE · REF 10

Noah's ketones were about 100 times the upper limit, and other energy markers (succinate, lactate and 2-hydroxybutyrate) were raised alongside them. Together, this points to real stress in how his body makes and uses energy, not just what he ate that day. Differences in energy metabolism like this are more common in children on the spectrum, and we treat it as one of Noah's top priorities.

Our plan: Dr. Jared's plan adds MitoActiv (CoQ10) to support how his cells make energy and Kid's Digest so his food is fully digested and used. Keep regular meals and snacks with protein and slow carbs, with no long gaps, especially overnight.

Being addressed

Toxins and mold

0.16mg/mL urine creatinine
valid range 0.25–2.16

Several mold toxins came back high. The urine sample was dilute, but Dr. Jared reviewed the results and found them still readable. Heavy metals were all in range.

Mold toxins are the first focus of Dr. Jared's plan: BioToxin Binder to clear them and KL Support to protect the liver and kidneys. Your current home has been checked and is clear, which matters most: Noah needs to stay away from mold while he recovers.

Dr. Jared's guidance for home

Stay clear of mold

Mold toxins are the first priority. Noah can't fully recover while still breathing in mold. Your current home has been checked and is clear. If you're ever unsure about a space, a home mold test (ERMI) or a professional inspection can confirm it.

Keep exposures low

Filter your water and air where you can, choose organic food, don't heat food or drinks in plastic, and avoid lawn and pesticide sprays.

Diet

Rita's plan is a great fit. Two tweaks: any corn should be organic, and edamame and other soy should be non-GMO.

Avoid glutathione for now

One of the mold toxins on Noah's test (gliotoxin) reacts badly with glutathione, so leave it out of his routine.

Monthly check-ins

Dr. Jared follows up about once a month to see how Noah is doing and decide the next steps.
